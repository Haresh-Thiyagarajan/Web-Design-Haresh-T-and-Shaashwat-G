# Surface: whole site (index.html and all pages)

Mode: Operate (visitor finds a resource). Direction pinned by user: modeled on the City of Sammamish website shell, warm palette, clean and friendly. concept-seed not run because the direction was pinned. Code-led build (user forbade generated images).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A warm civic directory. Keeps the city-site shell residents already know (alert banner, utility bar, logo plus nav, photo hero, icon quick-link row, information-rich footer). Refuses the cold municipal blue and the generic hero-plus-card-grid template.

OWN-WORLD: Palette from Sammamish Commons cedar siding and sunset over Lake Sammamish. Cedar #A4471C carries the shell (utility bar, primary buttons, active nav). Deep espresso #2B1B12 for the footer and ink. Amber #F2A93B for highlights and focus. Evergreen #2F6B4F for "next date" and saved states. White content ground with a warm tint band #FBF4EC. Bricolage Grotesque headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible body. Lucide line icons at 1.75 stroke. 10px radii.

STORY: Visitors see the five required categories right away, search across everything, filter, save entries to My List, and print it. Judges see the rubric's own words on every page name and an About page that maps each rubric item.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Crisis alert bar (988 and 2-1-1). Cedar utility bar with text-size, contrast, and My List. White header with wordmark and the 8-item nav. Split hero: cedar field on the left with H1 "Find help, programs, and things to do in Sammamish", a large search box, and popular-search chips. On the right, the Sammamish Landing photo in a framed plate with caption. Directly below, a row of five category quick links with icon and live count.

FIRST VIEWPORT, as built (cited adaptation): the controls (text size, contrast) sit in the espresso crisis bar and My List moved into the white header, which avoids two dark bars stacked on top of each other. The nav is a full-width cedar bar under the header, the same way the City of Sammamish site gives its main nav its own bar. The hero field is light sand (#FBF4EC), not cedar, so the large H1 and search read with maximum legibility. Cedar carries the shell through the nav bar, the brand mark, buttons, and the H1 accent word. The quick-link row is pulled up so all five categories show at 1440x900.

FORM: Pinned city-site shell, position 1 of 1, no seed key (direction pinned by user).
Signature interaction: My List. A Save button on every entry, a slide-in list, and a print-ready "Sammamish Resource Card".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
