A little about us

We are students at Tesla STEM High School in Redmond, Washington, and members of FBLA. We made this website for the 2026–2027 FBLA Chapter Website Design event to make it easier for our neighbors in Sammamish to find local organizations, programs, services, events, and resources in one place.

This website is a student project. It is not an official City of Sammamish website.
