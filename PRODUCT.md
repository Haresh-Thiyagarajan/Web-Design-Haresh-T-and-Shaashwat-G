# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No build step. Works offline from the file system (fonts, icons, and photos are stored in the repo). Hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Users

- Sammamish, WA residents looking for organizations, programs, services, events, and resources, including people who need help soon (food, counseling, legal aid) and people looking for things to do or ways to volunteer.
- FBLA Website Design judges, who see the site demoed by the team on laptop, tablet, and phone and score it against the 2026–27 rating sheet.

## Product Purpose

A community resource website for Sammamish that collects local organizations, programs, services, events, and resources in one clearly organized place. It was made for the FBLA 2026–27 Website Design event (topic: Community Resource Website) by Haresh T and Shaashwat G. Success means a visitor can find a relevant resource within seconds, and a judge can find every rubric requirement without hunting.

## Positioning

Every required content type has its own page, named exactly as the assignment names it, so the structure itself proves the requirements are met. Every entry cites its source.

## Operating Context

- Judges cannot click or scan anything. The team drives the demo in a 7-minute presentation.
- Venue internet may be unreliable, so the site must work fully offline.
- Up to three devices (laptop, tablet, phone) are used to show it works on multiple platforms.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: Home, Organizations, Programs, Services, Events, Resources, Sources, About. Page names and About-page section headings use the exact wording from the FBLA topic and rating sheet.
- Interactive features: site-wide search, topic filters, an event list with next-date calculation, a "My List" that saves entries and prints them, and accessibility controls (text size, high contrast).
- This is a student project, not an official City of Sammamish site. It must not use the city logo or imply affiliation.

## Brand Commitments

- Layout and structure are modeled on the official City of Sammamish website (utility bar, main navigation, photo hero, icon quick-link row, information-rich footer) but with a warm color palette instead of the city's colors.
- Clean and friendly.
- Photos are supplied by the team. Never use AI-generated images.

## Evidence on Hand

- Photos in `assets/img/` were supplied by the user (gathered from the web). See the Sources page for credits. Two watermarked photos (lake sunset © Gary Hamburgh, farmers market © 2017) were removed from the site and moved to ~/Downloads/fbla-unused-photos until permission is obtained.
- Organization facts were gathered from official sites (sammamish.us, kcls.org) and directories on 2026-10-21. They must be re-verified before competition.
- No testimonials, statistics, or usage numbers exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Rubric-obvious: every requirement is labeled with the rubric's own words and findable in seconds.
2. Cite everything: each entry shows where its information came from.
3. Fast and resilient: works offline and on old phones.
4. Plain language and accessibility before decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard access, visible focus, 4.5:1 text contrast, reduced-motion support, text-size and high-contrast controls, semantic landmarks and headings.
